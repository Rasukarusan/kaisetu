Checkout v2 is live

What changed, who is affected, and what to do about it.

Summary

The checkout flow now confirms payment in a single step. Card details are tokenized in the browser, so the server never sees a raw card number.

Who is affected

Every customer paying by card. Bank transfer and invoice flows are untouched.
